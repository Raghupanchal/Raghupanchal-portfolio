# Stalight Campus ERP & Stalight Sync

## Stalight Campus ERP
Stalight Campus is an enterprise-grade smart educational campus Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform. It digitizes over 15 distinct academic and administrative operations across colleges and universities:
- **Student Information System (SIS)**: Centralized lifecycle tracking from admission to graduation.
- **Faculty & Staff Management**: Workload allocation, timetable scheduling, and leave approvals.
- **Smart Attendance System**: Multi-mode attendance capture including biometric, digital roll-call, and automated deficit notifications.
- **Examination & Grading System**: Hall ticket generation, mark entry, continuous internal evaluation (CIE), and automated semester GPA (SGPA) calculation.
- **Fee Management & Invoicing**: Structured installment tracking, receipt generation, and payment gateway integration.

## Stalight Sync
Stalight Sync is a dedicated student placement preparation and technical assessment ecosystem designed to bridge academic curriculum with industry hiring benchmarks. It includes coding playgrounds, automated problem evaluation, mock interviews, and resume analysis tools.

## Technical Stack
- **Frontend**: React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Material UI
- **Backend**: Python, Django REST Framework, Node.js, RESTful APIs
- **Database**: PostgreSQL, Redis caching
